Signing in On each new phone, tablet, or browser, you'll start with the same default username and password, even if you've changed it elsewhere. Once you're inside, you can set a new one (top bar → 🔒 Change password) — it will be remembered on this device only.

How to use your carnet

  • Mon Journal — write a sentence (or a page) each day. Pick a mood, a theme, or a prompt — or just write freely.
  • Chercher — look up any word in English or French. Save the ones you love to your collection.
  • Ma Collection — your personal vocab bank. Filter by gender, type, or theme.
  • Bibliothèque — a new book suggestion each day, and a place to keep notes on books you've read.

Your privacy Everything you write stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no one else can see your entries — even if they have the link.

✦ Important — keeping your carnet safe Because your entries live only in this browser, they will be wiped if you clear your cache, browsing data, or site data. To keep your words safe:

  • ⇩ Export backup (top bar) — saves everything (vocab, journal, books) as a small file. Do this every week or so. You can ⇧ Import backup on any other phone, tablet, or computer to carry your carnet with you.
  • ⇩ Export as PDF (inside Mon Journal & Bibliothèque) — saves a beautiful printable copy of your entries. Lovely to keep, share, or print.

A gentle rhythm: back up once a week, and again before clearing your browser or switching devices. ❦
